## Full Findings

### High — AI/LLM readiness files are stale (`Issue H1`)
- **Files:** `static/llms.txt`, `static/articles-urls.txt`
- **Evidence:** `llms.txt` lists only `opencode-vscode-setup`, `opencode-skills-guide`, `hello-world`, `how-to-use-skelementor` (4 / 14 posts). It also links a deleted `/links/` page. `articles-urls.txt` same.
- **Impact:** AI engines, Discover-style crawlers and `llms.txt` consumers see a partial corpus; 10 posts unreferenced, `/links/` 404.
- **Fix:** generate both from the site data at build/deploy time (template or CI step), or update manually to include all 14 post URLs minus `/links/`.

### High — Deploy pipeline fails to notify search engines about new posts (`Issue H2`)
- **File:** `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` lines 107–114
- **Evidence:** the IndexNow payload still submits:
  - `https://f9xr.org/post/opencode-vscode-setup/`, `https://f9xr.org/post/opencode-skills-guide/`, `https://f9xr.org/post/hello-world/` — all three use `/post/` while the actual permalink is `/p/<slug>/` (permalinks.toml). Every URL 404s → zero indexation pings land.
  - No newer post (n8n, vibe, grokbot, tui, mcp, cloudflare, etc.) is ever added.
- **Impact:** fast-indexation intent defeated; only the (already old) homepage plus 404 URLs are pushed.
- **Fix:** build the URL list from the deploy artifact (e.g. from `sitemap.xml` or via a Hugo list partial), or simply submit `https://f9xr.org/sitemap.xml`. Ensure Google ping uses the live custom domain sitemap.

### High — Tag taxonomy bloat (`Issue H3`)
- **Evidence:** sitemap now carries **57 tag URLs** (vs 14 posts, 2 categories). Several posts use near-duplicate/over-keyworded tags:
  - `how-to-install-n8n-locally-on-pc`: 11 tags — `n8n`, `n8n local install`, `install n8n docker`, `self-hosted n8n`, `docker n8n`, `n8n npm`, `n8n windows`, `n8n macos`, `local development`, `workflow automation`, `docker-n8n`-equivalents.
  - `vibe-coder-vs-vibe-engineer`: 10 tags.
- **Impact:** crawl budget and index quality diluted by near-duplicate thin term pages; tag pages are `index,follow` with auto-generated (generic) descriptions.
- **Fix:** consolidate to 3–5 meaningful tags per post; for tags with ≤1 post, add `noindex` in `layouts/_partials/data/description.html`/robot handling (or per-term `_index.md`), and keep the sitemap lean.

### Medium — Internal linking (OpenCode hub cluster) (`Issue M4`)
- **Evidence (link graph, body only):**
  - Inbound: `opencode-skills-guide` (6), `ai-coding-agents-explained` (4), `opencode-vscode-setup`/`opencode-vs-cursor`/`opencode-tui`/`mcp` (3).
  - Outbound: `opencode-skills-guide` → **0** posts; `opencode-vscode-setup` → **0** posts; `hello-world` → 0; `skelementor` → 0.
- **Impact:** money pages are hubs (good inbound) but dead-end (0 outbound) → anchor text flow stops; Google's "related" suggestions absent because the theme doesn't render a related widget on posts (checked `article.html` — no related posts block).
- **Fix:** add a "You may also like" partial or in-content links between the 7 OpenCode posts (skills ↔ vscode ↔ mcp ↔ tui ↔ vs-cursor ↔ agents-explained ↔ vite) and to `vibe-coder-vs-vibe-engineer`.

### Medium — Skelementor post affiliate/hotlink risk (`Issue M5`)
- **Evidence:** `how-to-use-skelementor/index.md` contains 16+ external links all with `?via=f9xr` (skelementor.com, elementor.com), plus 5 `_next/image` remote images; cover/front-matter `image: https://skelementor.com/og-image.png` is **hotlinked** and becomes `og:image` (off-site). No affiliate disclosure text.
- **Impact:** trust/E-E-A-T risk if disclosure is required; cover image on remote domain can break/hotlink-serve slow; `og:image` points off-site.
- **Fix:** short-term: add explicit disclosure ("We may earn a commission on links"; optional); download `og-image.png` into the post bundle so `og:image` is local; keep remote gallery images but ensure `alt` maintained (already ✅).

### Medium — Thin posts below competitive threshold (`Issue M6`)
- `opencode-vs-cursor` (761), `hugo-github-pages-setup` (844), `hugo-seo-guide` (846), `ai-coding-agents-explained` (810) are 0.8–1.7k words vs the 2.4k+ competitor posts with comparison tables.
- **Fix:** merge a comparison table into `opencode-vs-cursor` (it's the intended "comparison" post — a table would add dimension + anchor scope), expand `hugo-*` posts with code/diff examples, and add a "How it works in practice" section to `ai-coding-agents-explained`.

### Medium — Author inconsistency on Cloudflare post (`Issue M7`)
- `github-pages-custom-domain-cloudflare/index.md` `author: dev9b` → JSON-LD renders `Person {"name":"dev9b"}` (no URL/sameAs), while every other post uses the brand `Organization`. Inconsistent E-E-A-T authorship.

### Low / Notes
- **2 H1s per page** — brand `<h1>` in sidebar + post `<h1>`: acceptable (Google permits multiple H1s), and the post title is correctly the first content H1. Optional cleanup.
- **Cover sizes:** `opencode-mcp-servers/cover.png` 609 KB, `opencode-skills-guide/cover.webp` 460 KB, `vibe-coder/cover.jpg` 392 KB — Server-derived `srcset` already minifies on render (built covers 3–36 KB) but original files inflate repo/image-processing time.
- **Date format:** `how-to-setup-grokbot` uses `"2026-09-14T16:10:00+05:30"` — fine.
- **`hello-world`** 16-char title tag — acceptable for intro, but could add `"F9XR Team"` target modifier.
- **Disqus on all articles** — third-party perf + GDPR. Optional.
- **robots.txt** correctly allows all AI bots (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, etc.) — good for AI/LLM SEO.
- **No `noindex` on `/tags/*` single-post pages** — smallest marginal fix.
